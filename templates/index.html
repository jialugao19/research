RESEARCH PHILOSOPHY

Operating principles

Research quality is defined by the decision process, not by the most favorable metric.

  1. 01

    Reproducibility

    Every experiment preserves its data scope, configuration, and evaluation rules.

  2. 02

    Evidence first

    Models advance only after robust validation; negative results remain part of the record.

  3. 03

    Execution aware

    Signals are evaluated under selection, turnover, cost, and out-of-sample constraints.
