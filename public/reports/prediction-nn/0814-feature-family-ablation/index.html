5. Interpretation and limits

The matched add-one-family evidence supports relative/liquidity, volatility/range, and trend/return as useful groups on this seed and split. It does not isolate individual fields within a family, prove cross-seed stability, or estimate higher-order interactions among the three passing families. The full63 result shows that individually useful groups do not necessarily compose monotonically. The market group has only an early gate outcome, so its test effect remains intentionally unknown.
